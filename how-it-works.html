How It Works

Learn how you can earn money by helping brands improve their websites

💰 Get Paid to Test Websites

Top brands and companies pay you to test and rate the navigability of their websites. They want to ensure their users have the best possible experience when browsing their sites. By sharing your honest feedback about how easy or difficult it is to navigate their website, you help them identify issues and improve their user experience.

Every test takes between 5-15 minutes and pays between $9-$18 per test. The more tests you complete, the more you can earn!

Follow the instructions for each test. You'll typically navigate through specific scenarios while sharing your thoughts out loud about the website's usability.

3

Get Paid

Once your test is reviewed and approved, payments are sent directly to your account. There's no minimum withdrawal amount!

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I get paid for testing websites?
After completing a test, your submission is reviewed. Once approved, the payment is added to your account. You can withdraw your earnings anytime with no minimum threshold.
How much can I earn per test?
Payments typically range from $9 to $18 per test, depending on the complexity of the task and the brand. Tests usually take 5-15 minutes to complete.
Do I need special equipment to test websites?
All you need is a computer with a stable internet connection and a microphone to record your thoughts. Most tests can be completed using any modern web browser.
How often are new tests available?
New tests are added regularly by our brand partners. We recommend checking the Available Sites page frequently to catch new opportunities as they become available.
What kind of websites will I be testing?
You'll test a variety of websites including e-commerce sites, social media platforms, streaming services, and corporate websites. Each test focuses on navigability and user experience.
Is there a minimum age requirement?
Yes, you must be at least 18 years old to participate in website testing. This is to ensure our testers can provide mature and thoughtful feedback.
How do brands use my feedback?
Brands use your feedback to identify usability issues, improve website navigation, optimize the user journey, and ultimately create better experiences for their visitors. Your input directly influences website improvements.
